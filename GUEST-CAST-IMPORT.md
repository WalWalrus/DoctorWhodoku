# Series 2–4 and Tennant specials: guest-cast import

Imported 18 November 2026. Scope: TVmaze regular/significant-special episodes dated after Series 1 and through 1 January 2010, including The End of Time – Part Two. Existing episodes outside this scope were retained. Insignificant-special credits (including compilation/presentation material) were not imported.

## Result

- 50 existing episode records matched; 47 contain guest credits.
- 401 new character records, of which 47 generic background roles are disabled pending individual review.
- 575 new episode links marked `unreviewed`, excluded from game eligibility until reviewed.
- All 206 previous appearance links and existing trivia facts preserved.
- Production labels containing “Operator” omitted; these are not extra characters.

## Review in the editor

Open **Check and download → Unreviewed guest-cast appearances**. Click an entry, check its scene, choose the correct Appearance mode, and save. In-person, voice and in-story broadcast count in the game; still images, archive footage and remains do not. A source voice flag is recorded as metadata, not treated as scene evidence. Background characters can be enabled individually if they are relevant.

This is a guest-cast import. Regular-cast episode appearances are not complete; do not infer that the Doctor or a companion appears just because they belong to the main cast. Add missing regular-cast links and in-universe trivia facts manually. Cast entries also may separate younger versions, disguises or identities that you prefer to merge; those require individual review.

TVmaze has no guest credits here for **Born Again**, **The Infinite Quest** or **Time Crash**. Their existing episode records remain available for manual linking.
